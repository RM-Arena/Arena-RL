# Dependencies

Arena-RL loads Arena-1 from a separate source checkout. Arena-1 physics, rules, SDK and assets remain covered by that repository's license and notices; they are not relicensed here.

Training uses Stable-Baselines3 (MIT), Gymnasium (MIT), NumPy (BSD-3-Clause), and PyTorch (BSD-style). Dependencies are installed separately through uv and retain their own license files and notices. No dependency source or simulator assets are redistributed in this repository.
